Context & Intent

Poorna Market is one of Visakhapatnam's oldest and most vibrant commercial markets. Over decades of organic growth, density and congestion have challenged its infrastructure and user experience. This project aims to organise, upgrade and reimagine the market as a modern, climate-responsive and inclusive community market, without losing its character and local identity.
